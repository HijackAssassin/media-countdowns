Reset

Resets the counters above and clears the cache — installation history ("Installations ever seen" and "Active in last 30 days") is never affected by this, no matter what. Right-click any individual counter's card to reset just that one instead (those don't touch the cache either). After a full reset, expect a burst of fresh upstream TMDB/IGDB calls as everything gets re-cached from scratch.
